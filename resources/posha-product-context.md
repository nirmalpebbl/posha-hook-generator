# Posha Product Context

## What is Posha?
Posha is an autonomous kitchen robot that cooks real meals for you. It's an AI-powered countertop cooking device that stirs, heats, and cooks automatically using 1000+ recipes.

## Key Value Props
- Fully autonomous cooking — no standing over the stove, no stirring, no timing
- 1000+ recipes built in, from weeknight dinners to elaborate sauces
- Works with fresh ingredients (not meal kits)
- AI adapts recipes to dietary needs, preferences, and what's in your fridge
- Clean up is minimal — the pot is the only thing to wash
- Results taste like home cooking, not like processed shortcuts

## Target Audience
- Busy parents who want to feed their family real food without hours in the kitchen
- Working professionals who are tired of takeout but don't have time to cook
- Home cooks who want to level up without culinary school
- Health-conscious people who want control over ingredients

## Tone & Positioning
- Not a gadget — it's a kitchen companion
- Not fast food — it's real, wholesome, delicious food
- Empowering: gives people back their time and confidence in the kitchen
- Modern, aspirational but relatable

## Hook Angles That Work for Posha
1. Time savings: "I used to spend 2 hours cooking dinner. Now I press a button."
2. Real food positioning: "No meal kits. No frozen shortcuts. Just real ingredients."
3. Social proof surprise: "My picky 7-year-old asked for seconds."
4. The reveal: Show the mess-free result, beautifully plated
5. Comparison: Before Posha vs. After Posha evening routine
6. Skeptic-to-believer: "I thought it was a gimmick. Then I tried it."
7. Specific recipe wow: "It made Thai green curry better than my favorite restaurant."
8. Lifestyle upgrade: "This is how busy people eat well."
