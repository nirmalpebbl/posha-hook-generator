# Hooks - Do’s and Don’ts

| **Do’s** | **Don’ts** |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep it relevant to your main content.** Make sure the hook aligns with the video’s theme and message. | **Avoid misleading or using clickbait.** Don’t use false claims just to get attention; it reduces credibility. |
| **Test different types of hooks.** Experiment with bold statements, questions, and visuals to see what works best for your audience. | **Don’t overcomplicate.** Avoid lengthy or confusing hooks that need extra context to understand. |
| **Stay on trend.** Use hooks that tap into [trending topics](https://trends.google.com/trends/) or viral formats in your niche. | **Don’t neglect visuals.** Make sure your visuals are as compelling as your text/audio hook. |
| **Create curiosity or suspense.** Leave a question unanswered or hint at valuable information that’s revealed later. | **Don’t drag out the hook.** A hook should be quick and to the point—don’t delay getting to the main content. |
| **Include a clear promise or benefit.** Let viewers know what value they’ll get if they keep watching. | **Don’t use generic openers.** Phrases like “Welcome to my channel” waste valuable seconds and don’t grab attention. |
| **Use engaging visuals and text.** Pair your hook with striking visuals or on-screen text that reinforces your message. | **Don’t reuse the same hook repeatedly.** Even successful hooks lose their impact with overuse—keep it fresh and varied. |
