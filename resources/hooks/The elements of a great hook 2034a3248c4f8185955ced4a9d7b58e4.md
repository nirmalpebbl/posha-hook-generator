# The elements of a great hook

---

## The elements of a great hook

Now, you’re probably wondering: what does a good hook include?

While there are stacks of ways to create a great Reel or TikTok, your hook should include:

- A punchy transition or eye-catching visual that grabs the attention of your viewer and stops them in their tracks.
- A clear value statement that articulates why this viewer should watch your content: emphasise the problem you’re solving or the solution you’re delivering!

```
      • Are you saving your audience time or money?

      • Are you teaching them an insider tip or trick?

      • Are you helping them work smarter, not harder?
```

- A call to action that directs them to keep watching, read your caption or engage with your content in some way to learn more.

---

## Why should you add a hook to your TikTok or Instagram Reels content?

So, what’s the point of adding a hook to your video content? 

The truth is that there is so many Reels and TikTok videos fighting for the eyeballs of your ideal customers.

In 2025, you can’t leave things to chance when it comes to engaging your audience!

Here’s why you need to prioritize a hook on every single TikTok or Reel you create:

- A hook taps into the needs, goals and aspirations of your ideal customer, ensuring they resonate with your content and dive in to learn more about your business or brand.
- A hook gives your audience a clear reason to stick around, boosting your engagement rate and giving all the right signs to the Instagram and TikTok gods!
- A hook quickly summarizes the key points or benefits of your content in a punchy, compelling and convincing way.

Ultimately, if you’re looking to boost your engagement rate, expand your reach and enhance your chances of landing on the Instagram Discover page, you need to be adding a hook to every video you create.
