# How to Write Great Hooks

---

### **1. Grab Attention in the First 3 Seconds**

- **Start with a Bang**: Use shocking statements, bold claims, or exciting visuals.
- The hook must stop viewers from scrolling.

**Examples**:

- “I can’t believe this actually works!”
- “Here’s why your morning routine is ruining your life.”

---

### **2. Leverage Curiosity and Intrigue**

- Pose a question or tease information to create an irresistible urge to watch.
- Avoid giving away too much; leave room for viewers to wonder.

**Examples**:

- “What if I told you everything you know about productivity is wrong?”
- “This one mistake could cost you thousands—don’t make it!”

---

### **3. Make It Relatable**

- Speak directly to your audience’s struggles, desires, or aspirations.
- Use words like *you* or phrases they frequently think about.

**Examples**:

- “Do you ever feel like no matter how hard you try, it’s never enough?”
- “We’ve all been there—wishing for more hours in a day. Here’s the fix.”

---

### **4. Tap Into Emotions**

- Viral hooks often evoke strong feelings—joy, surprise, anger, or nostalgia.
- Triggering emotions increases shareability.

**Examples**:

- “This story will break your heart—and then heal it.”
- “You’ll never look at your best friend the same way after hearing this.”

---

### **5. Offer Immediate Value**

- Promise viewers something they can learn or gain in a short time.
- Use “how-to” or “quick tips” style formats.

**Examples**:

- “In 30 seconds, I’ll teach you how to save $1,000 this month.”
- “Here’s the secret to glowing skin no one talks about.”

---

### **6. Use Lists and Numbers**

- Lists are highly engaging because they set clear expectations.
- Numbers make your hook precise and compelling.

**Examples**:

- “3 life-changing habits you can start today.”
- “5 things you’re doing wrong in the gym.”

---

### **7. Appeal to Trends and Pop Culture**

- Capitalize on trending topics, memes, or challenges.
- Use hashtags and references that are currently viral.

**Examples**:

- “I tried [Trending Challenge] and here’s what happened!”
- “If you’re not doing this, you’re missing out—seriously.”

---

### **8. Create FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)**

- Make viewers feel like they’ll regret not watching your reel.

**Examples**:

- “You’re going to wish you saw this sooner.”
- “Don’t scroll past this—your future self will thank you!”

---

### **9. Use Strong Commands**

- Start with action words to prompt engagement.
- Keep it simple and direct.

**Examples**:

- “Stop scrolling and listen to this!”
- “Watch this before it’s too late.”

---

### **10. Combine With Visual and Audio Cues**

- Use dynamic text overlays to emphasize key points.
- Pair your hook with engaging background music or sound effects.

---

### **11. Be Authentic and Bold**

- Authenticity is key in today’s content landscape. Speak naturally and let your personality shine.
- Don’t be afraid to take risks with edgy or unexpected statements.

**Examples**:

- “I’m about to spill the tea no one else will tell you.”
- “This is controversial, but I have to say it…”

---

### **12. Test, Tweak, Repeat**

- Monitor which hooks perform best through metrics like watch time and shares.
- Continuously experiment with new ideas and refine based on feedback.

## **Use “But & So” to Re-Hook Throughout the Video**

One great hook isn’t enough—you need to *keep* them engaged.

### How to Use It in Your Video:

1. Start with a surprising fact or statement.
2. Add a twist or question (“but…”).
3. Show a solution or resolution (“so…”).

### Examples of Mid-Video Re-Hooks:

- A surprising fact: “Most people get this completely wrong…”
- A thought-provoking question: “Have you ever wondered why…?”
- A shift in perspective: “But what if I told you…?”

This keeps viewers curious and engaged from start to finish.
