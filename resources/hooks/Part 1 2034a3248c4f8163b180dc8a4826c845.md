# Part 1

Question-Based Hooks part 1

| S.No | Actual Hook | Example Hook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Can I share a secret with you? | Can I share a secret about unlocking your potential? |
| 2 | Has this happened to you before? | Has this ever happened to you when trying to learn a new skill? |
| 3 | When’s the last time you _____? | When’s the last time you took a risk in your business? |
| 4 | Do you want to know why you should never ________ | Do you want to know why you should never ignore your health? |
| 5 | What if I told you {insert a polarizing statement} ... | What if I told you multitasking actually reduces productivity? |
| 6 | Have you ever noticed that [FAMOUS PERSON IN YOUR INDUSTRY][CHARACTERISTIC, ACTION, TRAIT]? Here's why... | Have you ever noticed that Steve Jobs always wore the same black turtleneck? Here's why... |
| 7 | If you're an entrepreneur on TikTok, I have a question for you... | If you're an entrepreneur on TikTok, I have a question for you about content consistency... |
| 8 | What would happen if you believed in (desirable outcome)? | What would happen if you believed in your ability to achieve financial freedom? |
| 9 | Do you have the ability to (desirable outcome) without (undesirable thing)? | Do you have the ability to lose weight without feeling deprived? |
| 10 | Are you making this mistake with [a specific task]? | Are you making this mistake with your morning routine? |
| 11 | Ever ask yourself, [thought-provoking question]? | Ever ask yourself, what if my biggest failure turned out to be my best opportunity? |
| 12 | Do you know who you spend the most time with in your life? | Do you know who you spend the most time with at work? |
| 13 | Can you guess why this [related to niche] is outcompeting x y z? | Can you guess why this new book is outcompeting every other self-help book right now? |
| 14 | Can you spot the difference in [items]? | Can you spot the difference in these two marketing strategies? |
| 15 | Do you want to know how to [achieve something]? | Do you want to know how to boost your productivity by 50% in 30 days? |
| 16 | Do you want to know how to double your savings this year? | Do you want to know how to double your savings this year without cutting back on your lifestyle? |
| 17 | Do you know the secret to [achieving something]? | Do you know the secret to building a brand that stands out? |
| 18 | Here’s why I disagree with [common belief]… | Here’s why I disagree with the belief that overnight success is possible... |
| 19 | Here’s why I’ll never stop ___. | Here’s why I’ll never stop investing in personal growth. |
| 20 | If you [do this], I automatically assume [desired outcome]. | If you work on your mindset daily, I automatically assume you're headed for success. |
| 21 | If you agree, hit the like button… | If you agree that creativity is the key to business growth, hit the like button! |
| 22 | If you can relate to this, keep watching. | If you can relate to the struggle of building your own business, keep watching. |
| 23 | If you do x, you’re going to [negative result]. | If you procrastinate, you’re going to miss your big opportunity. |
| 24 | If you’re still [doing this], I’m begging you to listen to me… | If you’re still undercharging for your services, I’m begging you to listen to me... |
| 25 | If you’re under (# of age) and you’re not x, listen up! | If you’re under 30 and you’re not investing in yourself, listen up! |
| 26 | If you’ve ever struggled with x, do x. | If you’ve ever struggled with time management, try setting specific goals. |
| 27 | I want to be extremely honest with you about [topic]. | I want to be extremely honest with you about how hard it is to start a business. |
| 28 | I was skeptical about [method], but this really works. | I was skeptical about using a morning routine, but this really works. |
| 29 | Here’s why I stopped [common practice]. | Here’s why I stopped checking emails first thing in the morning. |
| 30 | Here’s why I’m obsessed with [product/trend]. | Here’s why I’m obsessed with productivity apps for my daily routine. |
| 31 | Here’s why I’ll never ___ again. | Here’s why I’ll never skip my workout routine again. |
| 32 | If you’re thinking about [action], watch this first. | If you’re thinking about launching a product, watch this first. |
| 33 | If you’re struggling with [problem], watch this. | If you’re struggling with writer’s block, watch this. |
| 34 | Is it just me, or does [something strange] happen to everyone? | Is it just me, or does time seem to fly when you’re working on a passion project? |
| 35 | Is there anything worse than x? | Is there anything worse than not having a backup plan? |
| 36 | Let me know in the comments what you think… | Let me know in the comments what you think about the new product trend! |
| 37 | Let’s figure out why (pain point to target audience) | Let’s figure out why productivity seems impossible for busy entrepreneurs. |
| 38 | Let’s talk about overcoming mistakes I made when [situation or activity]. | Let’s talk about overcoming mistakes I made when trying to juggle multiple projects. |
| 39 | How would you react if [something surprising] happened? | How would you react if you woke up to find out your business doubled overnight? |
| 40 | How would you react if you could become fluent in a new language in one month? | How would you react if you could finish your dream project by the end of this month? |
